Personal project · 2026

Discarded
Toast

Web app UX design Illustration React

A mood-based digital diary app built for fun. On good days you write a happy toast- a small note delivered back to you on another good day as a reminder of what's already been. On harder days you write a hopeful toast, sent forward to a day that needs it from a past you who was also pushing through hard times.

Private toasts are yours alone. Public ones appear on a shared wall, completely anonymous.

Personal project · 2025

Cassette
Tape Book

Bookbinding Laser cutting Craft

A handbound book designed around a cassette tape. The covers are laser cut, and the cogs on the front are functional - they spin to allow the bookmark to retract, mimicking the tape mechanism.

Made over the summer while learning to bookbind. Thanks to Neil Shepherd for teaching me bookbinding (among other things), and to Leyre Istúriz for taking me cassette tape hunting to MONO.
